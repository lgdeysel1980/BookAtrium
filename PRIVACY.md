# BookAtrium Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 18 August 2026

BookAtrium is a Windows ebook and audiobook library application published by **PractiCore**.

This Privacy Policy explains how BookAtrium accesses, processes, stores, and transmits information when you use the application, its optional online features, plugins, and related support services.

BookAtrium is designed primarily as a **local desktop application**. Your library database, ebooks, audiobooks, covers, reading information, and other library data are normally stored and processed on your own computer or in storage locations that you choose.

## 1. Information BookAtrium Processes Locally

BookAtrium may access and process information that you provide to or manage through the application, including:

- Ebook and audiobook files.
- Book titles, authors, publishers, descriptions, identifiers, categories, tags, ratings, series information, and other metadata.
- Book covers and other images.
- File and folder names and locations.
- Reading positions, bookmarks, reading progress, and reader settings.
- Audiobook playback positions and chapter information.
- Search history, saved searches, virtual libraries, and filters.
- Custom fields, notes, and other information that you add to your library.
- Library database files, backups, recycle-bin data, and application settings.
- Information imported from another library or application, including Calibre libraries where you choose to perform an import.

This information may contain personal or confidential information depending on the files and information that you choose to store.

By default, this library information is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to PractiCore merely because you use BookAtrium.

BookAtrium does not require a PractiCore account to manage your local library.

## 2. Information Sent Over the Internet

Some BookAtrium features require an internet or network connection. Information is transmitted only as necessary to provide the feature that you use.

### Metadata and book information lookups

BookAtrium includes or supports plugins that can retrieve book, author, cover, store, or other metadata from third-party websites and services.

When you use these features, BookAtrium may send information such as:

- Book titles.
- Author names.
- ISBNs.
- ASINs or other book identifiers.
- Search terms.
- Other metadata necessary to perform the requested lookup.

The third-party service will also normally receive standard network information associated with an internet request, such as your IP address, request headers, and technical connection information.

Examples of services that may be accessed by BookAtrium plugins include book retailers, metadata providers, search providers, image providers, and public book databases.

The availability of individual providers may change as BookAtrium and its plugins are updated.

### Store integrations

BookAtrium may include plugins that allow you to search or interact with third-party ebook stores or websites.

Information submitted through those integrations is transmitted directly to the relevant third-party service as necessary to perform the action that you requested.

BookAtrium does not control the privacy practices of those services.

### Application and plugin updates

BookAtrium may connect to official distribution or hosting services to:

- Check whether an application update is available.
- Retrieve release information.
- Download updates where applicable.
- Retrieve information about available community plugins.
- Download a plugin that you choose to install.

Depending on how BookAtrium was installed, these services may include Microsoft Store services, GitHub, or another official BookAtrium distribution service.

Those services may receive standard technical information associated with the network request, including your IP address and information normally transmitted by your device or network client.

## 3. Optional Web Library and Network Access

BookAtrium includes optional functionality that can make your library available through a web or OPDS-style interface.

This feature is not required for normal local use.

If you enable web-library or remote-access functionality, information from your BookAtrium library may be transmitted from your computer to devices or users that connect to the web server.

Depending on the configuration that you choose, this can include:

- Book metadata.
- Covers.
- Library organisation information.
- Ebook or audiobook files.
- Other information made available through the web interface.

This communication takes place between your BookAtrium installation and the connecting client. PractiCore does not receive a copy of your library merely because you use the web-library feature.

You are responsible for deciding whether to enable network or remote access and for configuring access appropriately for your environment.

BookAtrium may support networking features such as local-network access, port configuration, or configured dynamic-DNS services. If you use an external networking or dynamic-DNS service, that service may process your network address or other technical information according to its own privacy policy.

You should not expose your BookAtrium library to an untrusted network or the public internet without appropriate security controls.

## 4. External Links and Web Content

Ebooks, metadata, plugins, and other content may contain links to external websites.

If you choose to open an external link, your browser or other web component may connect directly to that website. The website may receive information such as your IP address and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own privacy policy.

BookAtrium does not control third-party websites.

## 5. Plugins

BookAtrium supports both official BookAtrium plugins and independently developed third-party plugins.

Plugins may access BookAtrium information or network services when necessary to provide their stated functionality.

Official plugins that communicate with third-party websites or services may send relevant search terms, book identifiers, metadata, or other information required for the feature that you request.

Third-party plugins are developed and distributed by independent developers. Their privacy and data-handling practices are the responsibility of their respective developers.

Before installing a third-party plugin, you should review information provided by its developer and install plugins only from sources that you trust.

PractiCore does not control data collection or processing performed independently by third-party plugins.

## 6. Diagnostic and Support Information

BookAtrium may create diagnostic information locally, such as application logs and error information, for troubleshooting purposes.

PractiCore does not require you to submit your library files, database, diagnostic logs, screenshots, or other private material in order to use BookAtrium.

If you voluntarily provide information when requesting support or reporting a problem, PractiCore will receive the information that you choose to provide.

This may include:

- Your BookAtrium version.
- Your Windows version.
- Error messages.
- Diagnostic logs.
- Screenshots.
- Steps to reproduce a problem.
- Other technical information that you include in your report.

You should review diagnostic files, screenshots, and other attachments before submitting them and remove information that you do not want to disclose.

Do not submit passwords, licence keys, private ebooks, personal documents, database backups, financial information, authentication credentials, signing certificates, API credentials, or other confidential information through public support channels.

## 7. GitHub Support and Community Services

The official BookAtrium GitHub repository is used for activities such as:

- Software support.
- Bug reports.
- Feature requests.
- Documentation feedback.
- Community discussions.
- Release information.
- Application downloads for applicable distribution channels.

Information that you post in a public GitHub Issue or Discussion can be viewed by other people and may remain publicly accessible.

GitHub processes information according to GitHub's own terms and privacy practices.

Do not place personal, confidential, or sensitive information in a public GitHub Issue or Discussion.

For security or privacy matters that require disclosure of sensitive information, use a private contact method rather than posting the information publicly.

See the [BookAtrium Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for security-reporting guidance.

## 8. Information PractiCore Does Not Sell or Use for Advertising

PractiCore does not sell BookAtrium users' personal information.

BookAtrium does not use your library contents or reading activity for targeted advertising.

BookAtrium does not require access to your personal library for advertising purposes.

## 9. Storage and Retention

### Information stored locally

Your BookAtrium library and related information remain on your device or in storage locations that you select until you modify, move, delete, or otherwise manage that information.

BookAtrium may maintain local backups, recycle-bin information, indexes, application settings, and other supporting information as part of its library-management features.

You control these files through BookAtrium and your operating system.

Uninstalling BookAtrium may not automatically delete user-created libraries, books, backups, or other files that you deliberately stored outside the application's installation area.

### Information provided for support

Information that you voluntarily provide to PractiCore for support, security reporting, or other communication may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Respond to your request.
- Investigate and resolve a technical or security problem.
- Maintain appropriate support or security records.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

Information submitted to third-party services such as GitHub is also subject to those services' retention policies.

## 10. Security

BookAtrium is designed to keep its primary library data under the user's control on the user's own device.

PractiCore takes reasonable measures to protect information that it receives and to design BookAtrium so that network functionality is used only for legitimate application features.

However, no software, computer system, network connection, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your Windows account, computer, storage devices, backups, home or business network, and any remote-access configuration that you enable.

## 11. Your Choices and Controls

You control many of BookAtrium's data-processing activities directly.

Depending on the feature, you can:

- Choose which books and files to import.
- Edit or delete library metadata.
- Delete books and formats.
- Manage local backups.
- Choose whether to use metadata or store lookups.
- Choose whether to install or enable plugins.
- Remove third-party plugins where permitted.
- Choose whether to use external links.
- Choose whether to enable the optional web library or remote access.
- Control your own library files through Windows and your selected storage locations.
- Decide what information to include when contacting support.

Where information is stored only in your local BookAtrium library, PractiCore does not possess that information and therefore cannot access, correct, export, or delete it on your behalf. You can manage that information directly through BookAtrium or your computer.

## 12. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used with BookAtrium operate independently from PractiCore.

Their data practices may differ from this Privacy Policy.

This includes, where applicable:

- Microsoft and Microsoft Store services.
- GitHub.
- Book retailers.
- Metadata providers.
- Search and image providers.
- Dynamic-DNS or networking providers.
- Websites opened from BookAtrium.
- Services used by independently developed third-party plugins.

When you use a third-party service, its own privacy policy and terms govern the information processed by that service.

## 13. International Data Processing

BookAtrium can be used in multiple countries and regions.

When you choose to use an internet-based third-party service, information sent to that service may be processed in a country different from your own.

The location and safeguards applicable to that processing are governed by the relevant third-party provider and applicable law.

BookAtrium's local library data is not transferred internationally by PractiCore merely because you use the application.

## 14. Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights concerning personal information held by PractiCore, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or information about how personal information is processed.

These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

Because most BookAtrium library information is stored locally and is not held by PractiCore, you normally exercise control over that information directly on your own device.

For information that you have provided directly to PractiCore, you may contact us using the method described below.

## 15. Children

BookAtrium is a general-purpose ebook and audiobook library application and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

BookAtrium does not require users to create a PractiCore account to maintain a local library.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of third-party websites, plugins, online metadata services, and any remote-access features.

Third-party services may apply their own age requirements and privacy practices.

## 16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BookAtrium will continue to develop over time.

PractiCore may update this Privacy Policy when:

- BookAtrium functionality changes.
- New online services are introduced.
- Data-handling practices change.
- New legal or regulatory requirements apply.
- New distribution channels or commercial features are introduced.

The current version of this Privacy Policy will be published with the official BookAtrium project information.

If a future BookAtrium feature materially changes the way personal information is collected, used, or shared, the Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly and additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

## 17. Contact and Privacy Requests

BookAtrium is published by **PractiCore**.

General BookAtrium information and support are available through the official [BookAtrium repository](README.md).

For general product support, use the support options provided through the BookAtrium repository or Microsoft Store listing.

For a privacy request that requires you to provide personal or confidential information, **do not post that information in a public GitHub Issue or Discussion**. Use the private support or contact method identified by PractiCore or request an appropriate private contact channel before providing sensitive information.

When submitting a privacy request, provide only the information reasonably necessary for us to understand and respond to the request.
